# FitFlow Privacy Policy

**Version 1.1 · Effective 4 October 2026 · Applies to FitFlow 1.1.0 for Android and iOS**

FitFlow ("we", "us") is a fitness and nutrition app developed as part of the IT3060 Human Computer
Interaction coursework at SLIIT, Malabe, Sri Lanka. This policy explains what information FitFlow
uses, where it is kept, and the choices and rights you have. We have written it in plain language;
if anything is unclear, contact us using the details in section 11.

## 1. Summary

- **Everything stays on your phone.** In version 1.1.0, FitFlow has no servers and no internet
  access. Your profile, workouts, meals and posts are stored only on your device.
- **No accounts, no ads, no tracking.** We do not sell, share or rent your information, and the app
  contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.
- **Photos are analysed on your phone and then deleted.** Meal photos are never uploaded.
- **You are in control.** You can export or delete all of your data from Settings at any time.

## 2. Information FitFlow uses

| Information | Examples | Why it is used |
|---|---|---|
| Profile | First name (optional), goal, training level, session length, weekly goal, equipment | Personalise your daily plan |
| Health and fitness information | Injuries you choose to flag, energy level, workouts, sets, weights, personal bests | Plan safe workouts, show progress |
| Nutrition information | Meals, foods, portions, calories and macronutrients | Show your daily intake |
| Meal photos | A photo you take or choose | Recognise foods on the device; deleted when you leave the review screen |
| Posts | Text you share in a circle, likes | Show your post to the circle you choose |
| Consent record | Whether you agreed to health-data processing | Prove consent was given (GDPR Art. 7) |

FitFlow does **not** use your contacts, location, microphone, advertising ID or device identifiers.
The camera and photo library are used only when you choose to snap or pick a meal photo, through your
phone's standard camera and picker.

## 3. Health information and your consent

Fitness, injury and nutrition information is **health data**, a special category of personal data
under the EU/UK GDPR (Article 9) and sensitive data under Sri Lanka's Personal Data Protection Act,
No. 9 of 2022. FitFlow processes it only with your **explicit consent**, which you give by ticking
the consent box during set-up. You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings → *Health data
consent*; withdrawing deletes your data, because FitFlow cannot work without it.

## 4. AI and automated processing

- **Workout planner.** Your daily plan is produced by a rule-based planner that runs on your
  phone. It reads your goal, level, session length, today's energy, equipment, injuries and last
  session. Each plan shows the reasons behind it ("Why this workout?"), and you can change the
  duration, difficulty or individual exercises, or generate a new plan.
- **Food recognition.** Meal photos are analysed on your phone. Each recognised item shows a
  confidence level and nothing is saved until you have checked it.
- These features make suggestions only. They do not make decisions with legal or similarly
  significant effects (GDPR Article 22), and they are not medical advice.

## 5. Social features (circles)

- New posts go to your **private circle** by default. Every post and the post composer state exactly
  who can see the post (for example, "Visible to 5 members of Training partners").
- You can delete your own posts and report other people's posts.
- In version 1.1.0, circles are a local preview: posts are stored on your device and are not sent to
  other people. When online circles are introduced, this policy will be updated **before** the
  change takes effect and you will be asked to agree to it.

## 6. Where your data is stored and how it is protected

Your data is stored in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. It
is protected by your device's own security (screen lock and encryption). It is removed if you delete
the app or use *Delete all my data*. Because nothing is sent to us, we cannot see, recover or restore
your data.

## 7. Sharing

We do not share your information with anyone. There are no third-party analytics, advertising or
data brokers in FitFlow 1.1.0.

Future versions (described in our technical roadmap) may add an optional account, cloud backup and
crash reporting (Google Firebase Crashlytics). If they do, this policy will list each provider, what
it receives and why, before the release that adds it.

## 8. Retention

Data stays on your device until you delete it. Meal photos are deleted as soon as you leave the
meal review screen, whether or not you save the meal.

## 9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR or Sri Lanka's PDPA), you have the
right to:

| Right | How to use it in FitFlow |
|---|---|
| Access and data portability | Settings → *Export my data* (copies all your data as a JSON file) |
| Correction | Edit your profile in Settings; edit or remove meals and posts |
| Erasure | Settings → *Delete all my data*, or uninstall the app |
| Withdraw consent | Settings → *Health data consent* |
| Object / restrict processing | Turn off health-data consent or stop using a feature |
| Complain | Contact us first; you may also complain to your data protection authority (in Sri Lanka, the Data Protection Authority; in the UK, the ICO; in the EU, your national authority) |

## 10. Children

FitFlow is intended for people aged 16 and over and is not directed at children. We do not
knowingly process information about children under 13.

## 11. HIPAA

FitFlow is not a healthcare provider, health plan or healthcare clearing house, and it does not
process information on behalf of one, so the US Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act
(HIPAA) does not apply to it. We nevertheless follow HIPAA-style safeguards for health information:
data minimisation, on-device processing, access limited to the device owner, and secure deletion.

## 12. Medical disclaimer

FitFlow provides general fitness and nutrition information. It is not a medical device and does not
diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. Talk to a qualified professional before starting a new
exercise or nutrition programme, especially if you have a health condition or injury.

## 13. Changes to this policy

We will post any change here with a new version number and effective date, and show a notice in the
app. Material changes — such as sending data off your device — require your agreement first.
